# Nuvio Reshaped privacy policy

Nuvio Reshaped is a personal, unofficial fork of the Nuvio apps for Android phones and Android TV. This policy covers its optional "Sync with Google" feature.

## What Sync with Google does
When you sign in with Google under Settings > Nuvio Reshaped > Sync, the app keeps one file, "Nuvio Reshaped sync.json", in your own Google Drive. The file holds only the things you choose to sync:
- Nuvio Reshaped settings.
- Live TV sources and their logins, favourites, categories and the last channel you watched.

## Google data the app uses
- **Your email address** (`email` and `openid` scopes). The app uses it only to show which account is signed in.
- **Google Drive, files this app created** (`drive.file` scope). The app reads and writes only the sync file it made. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.

## Where your data goes
Your data goes only between your devices and your own Google account. There is no Nuvio Reshaped server. The developer never receives, stores or sees your data, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

## Removing your data
- Sign out under Settings > Nuvio Reshaped > Sync. This also withdraws the app's access to your Google account.
- To remove the synced data, delete "Nuvio Reshaped sync.json" from your Google Drive.
- You can also remove the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
